Triaged all 187 Carnegie R1 institutions for dedicated GIS librarian positions, using an ETL pipeline built on the Carnegie list, the ROR API, ARL membership data, and crawls of library staff directories, followed by manual verification.

The report pairs the directory with a case study of UCLA's geospatial research and the gap left when the campus cut its dedicated GIS librarian. In August 2026 UCLA committed to hiring a GIS librarian again; the report carries an update noting that.

Code and data: [jt14den/gis-librarians-library](https://github.com/jt14den/gis-librarians-library).
