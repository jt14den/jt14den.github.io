Built for the 12-week One IT Research Working Group, charged by UCLA's EVCP and CIO to assess campus research technology infrastructure. I represented the Library and the Data Science Center on the group.

The site collects the evidence from UCLA's earlier Research Data Working Group (2021-2024) and maps it to the working group's charge. It makes the case for four priorities: a research data coordination office, a Bruin Research Data Hub for storage, expanded research data services, and expanded research infrastructure (HPC, a P3/P4 compliant enclave, cloud for research). The core argument is that research IT is not generic IT, and that a layered model, with central infrastructure raising the floor and local expertise kept close to researchers, works better than consolidation.
